# cash market data fields

## Formatting hints

We have a special mix of many market data fields. The rendering and formatting is handled in [headerMapping.tsx](./headerMapping.tsx).

Here is a table with a brief overview of most of the fields plus an attempt to define their formatting rules.
Numbers are **generally** respecting the correct format for **de_CH** locale (123'456.78) and are right aligned.

There are differences between formatting rules of **number**, **value** and **price** formatting category. Numbers are usually just a number, mainly representing volume or quantity of some sort whereas
price represents usually a price in relation to finance instruments such as last, open, close, bid, ask etc.. Values are usually results of mathematical operations.
